EXTENDED NASA BATTERY SOH / RUL TRAINING

Dataset
- 34 unique batteries: the original four plus 30 newly added batteries.
- Duplicate B0025-B0028 files were deduplicated.
- Cycle time series are stored individually in tmp/ and indexed by metadata.csv.
- Feature extraction produced 2,794 discharge-cycle rows across 34 batteries.
- SOH training uses 2,513 rows with capacities in the 0.5-2.5 Ah range.

Labels and data quality
- SOH = measured discharge capacity / 2.0 Ah * 100.
- RUL EOL limits follow the README for each battery group (1.4 or 1.6 Ah).
- RUL labels require an observed, sustained EOL crossing and at least 10
  cycles of usable pre-EOL data. Invalid low-capacity records are not treated
  as measured battery health.
- 12 batteries meet the RUL-label requirements. Batteries without a documented
  EOL threshold, software-crashed experiments, and batteries with insufficient
  usable capacity history are excluded from supervised RUL training.
- Capacity cutoffs, included batteries, and excluded batteries are recorded in
  results.json and the saved model.

Models and validation
- SOH: Ridge regression, evaluated by leave-one-battery-out (LOBO) and
  chronological 60/40 splits.
- RUL: gradient boosting on out-of-fold predicted SOH trajectory features,
  evaluated with the same split strategies.
- LOBO mean battery MAE: SOH 4.58 percentage points; RUL 24.12 cycles.
- Chronological mean battery MAE: SOH 4.20 percentage points; RUL 31.60 cycles.
- RUL generalization is weak on this heterogeneous expanded dataset. Do not
  interpret the trained RUL model as production-ready; improve validation,
  EOL harmonization, and protocol handling before relying on its predictions.
- See results.json for per-battery MAE/RMSE/R2 and results/plots/ for charts.

Reproduce from the repository root:
1. Run all cells in nasa_battery_life_prediction_dataset_cleaning.ipynb when
   adding newly uploaded MATLAB battery folders.
2. python src/prep.py
3. python src/final.py
